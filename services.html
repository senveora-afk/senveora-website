FAQ

Questions, answered.

How does Results as a Service work?
We agree one measurable result and its target before we start. You pay a base fee for the build, plus a success bonus only when the result is measured and met. Read more about RaaS.
How long does a typical project take?
A focused automation or GenAI prototype usually takes 3–6 weeks. Production MLOps platforms and SaaS products depend on scope, typically 6–16 weeks. You'll get a clear timeline after our discovery call.
Do you work with small businesses and startups?
Absolutely. We work with startups, SMEs and growing enterprises, and tailor the solution and budget to where you are today.
Will I own the code and data?
Yes. Everything we build for you, including source code, designs and data, belongs to you.
Do you provide support after launch?
Yes. We offer flexible support and maintenance plans covering updates, monitoring, security and continuous improvement.
Can you work with our existing systems?
Definitely. We regularly integrate with existing CRMs, accounting software, payment gateways and legacy databases.
